# World Thermostat, 2026-11-24

## World temp: Hot

- **Iran / Hormuz (rising):** Iran's navy says two more tankers hit mines east of Hormuz. Tallies in other reports run as high as four, and no ships are named (Tasnim, Anadolu). That claim is **unverified**. UKMTO separately confirmed that three eastbound tankers were hit by unknown projectiles on Nov 23 and one more on Nov 22. All crews are accounted for and the ships kept sailing. Trump says no US-Iran talks are underway or scheduled and the blockade stays in place.
- **Ukraine / Russia (rising):** Zelensky says Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, and hit the cargo ship Angara (AP, Reuters). The war is now spilling into the Mediterranean. Putin calls retaliation for strikes on Russian oil plants "inevitable." Russian attacks killed 9 across Ukraine over the past day, 2 of them in a Zaporizhzhia apartment strike that wounded 48 (Kyiv Independent, AP).
- **Poland / NATO (steady):** Poland summoned Russia's envoy over the three Kh-101 missiles that crashed on its territory and reported a Russian drone briefly in its airspace on Nov 23. The NATO AWACS deployment stands (Polish MFA, Reuters).

Djibouti has taken in about 3,840 Yemenis fleeing the fighting since September, mostly at Obock and Markazi camp, where cholera has been reported (Mixed Migration Centre, The Conversation).
- **Taiwan (falling):** Taiwan's defense ministry tracked 3 PLA aircraft (all across the median line) and 9 ships, down from 6 aircraft and 12 ships.
- **Fuel and freight:** Brent settled at $91.40, up 2.27% as the Hormuz strikes continued (Morningstar/Dow Jones, CNBC). The US gas average is $3.61, down slightly (AAA).
- **Outbreak watch:** The DRC Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak is at 11,613 confirmed cases and 5,678 deaths, and it's still a WHO global emergency (WHO, data to Nov 17). Steady.
- **Florida hazards (rising):** The St. Johns River at Sanford is about 8.2 ft, above major flood stage, with a forecast crest of 8.4 ft and high water through the weekend. Some evacuations have happened (NWS, WUSF, FOX 35).
- **Market odds:** Polymarket priced "Israel x Iran ceasefire continues through Nov 30" at about 93% yes. That's a search snapshot, not checked live.

## Cyber front

- **Up to 50 commercial ships hacked (rising):** The US is investigating cyberattacks on as many as 50 vessels carrying oil, LNG, chemicals and other hazardous cargo. Reports say attackers got in through FortiGate firewall flaws, sometimes with bomb threats or ransom notes, and Coast Guard cyber teams have boarded at least four ships headed for US ports (NPR, WSJ via Reuters). The probe is **verified**. Iran is the prime suspect and Russia is also being looked at, so attribution stays **unverified**.
- **AI hacking tool tied to two government breaches (rising):** Hackers got into an unclassified open-source-intelligence system used by the CIA and ODNI. No classified systems were touched (AP, Nextgov). Investigators suspect the "Artex" AI vulnerability-finding tool, the same tool the European Commission says was used in its ongoing breach (Euronews). The breaches are **verified**. A possible China link, which Euronews' sources call a group codenamed "Panda," is **unverified**.
- **China-linked vCenter zero-day (steady):** Google Threat Intelligence says the group ARMORY CASTLE has exploited a VMware vCenter authentication bypass (CVE-2026-123890) since early September to take over admin sessions. Broadcom's patch is VMSA-2026-0009. **Verified**. CISA also added three entries to its exploited-vulnerability catalog, including a Drupal Core installer remote-code-execution flaw, with a federal deadline of Nov 27 (CISA).
- **FBI NCIC breach (steady):** The FBI now says it is actively investigating but won't say who did it or what was taken. Local agencies report the database still works normally (CyberScoop, Houma Today). That an investigation exists is **verified**. The scope is unknown.
- **Qilin claims Ipsos (steady):** The Qilin ransomware gang listed the polling firm Ipsos on its leak site with five sample documents. Ipsos confirms a breach of one internal system but not ransomware or Qilin (BreachNews, Ipsos). The leak-site claim is **unverified**.

## Prep tip

Wound packing beats a tourniquet where a tourniquet can't go: the groin, armpit and neck. Pack gauze tight to the bottom of the wound, then hold hard pressure for at least 3 minutes with hemostatic gauze, or about 10 minutes with plain gauze. Don't peek. Action: get a wound-packing trainer and practice packing a full roll under a timer until you can do it by feel.
