# World Thermostat, 2026-11-22

## World temp: Hot

- **Iran / Hormuz (rising):** Iran's IRGC Navy says two more tankers hit mines and caught fire east of the strait. It names no ships and gives no casualties, and no maritime authority has confirmed it. Separately, UKMTO reports a "tanker and military forces" incident in the Gulf of Oman, and ABC says it isn't clear whether the two are the same. Bahrain says an Iranian strike on Saturday set a civilian industrial site on fire and injured 2. The IRGC also claims it shot down a US MQ-1, which CENTCOM hasn't commented on. IMF PortWatch logged 2 transits on Nov 15, against a normal 85 a day.
- **Bab el-Mandeb / Djibouti / Camp Lemonnier (steady):** Houthis are fortifying Dhubab, the Kahbub heights and Mayun (Perim) Island with trenches, snipers and radar (The New Arab, TALi). A Houthi drone headed for Riyadh and a missile aimed at western Saudi Arabia were intercepted. No attacks or alerts were reported at Lemonnier. A viral claim that Houthis are "20 miles from the US base in Djibouti" is false: Perim sits about 80 miles from the base (Stars and Stripes).
- **Poland / NATO (rising):** Polish prosecutors say all three objects that crashed in Poland on Nov 20 were Russian Kh-101 missiles. No one was hurt. NATO's North Atlantic Council meets Monday (Reuters).
- **Ukraine (steady):** Russia launched 186 drones overnight and Ukraine says it downed 161. A strike on an apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv, killed at least 3, with people still missing. Zelensky counts about 2,700 drones and 165 missiles this week (AP, Kyiv Independent).
- **Taiwan (falling):** Taiwan's defense ministry tracked 24 PLA aircraft (17 across the median line) and 11 ships in the 24 hours to Sunday morning. The next 24 hours fell to 6 aircraft and 12 ships.
- **Fuel and freight:** Brent settled at $92.57 on Friday and reopened Sunday night near $89.4-89.8, down about 3% on talk of a deal and rising US crude inventories. The US gas average is $3.64 (AAA), down from $3.66.
- **Outbreak watch:** DRC Bundibugyo Ebola is at 11,613 confirmed cases and 5,678 deaths (WHO, as of Nov 17). WHO kept the international emergency in place on Nov 18. Steady.
- **Space weather:** Quiet, with no NOAA G-scale storm forecast.
- **Florida hazards (rising):** The St. Johns River is at major flood stage at Cocoa (above its previous record), Astor, Geneva and DeLand, and DeLand may approach its record. Sanford is forecast to reach major stage midweek. Coastal flood warnings run for northeast Florida until 5 AM Monday (NWS).
- **Market odds:** Polymarket has a US-Iran "effective ceasefire" by Nov 30 at 56.5%, up from roughly 49% last night, and by Dec 31 at 71%.

## Cyber front

- **Icelandic host 1984 knocked offline (rising):** The free-speech hosting firm shut down all services after what it calls a sophisticated attack, and NetBlocks confirmed the outage. CERT-IS says AI tools were used. Verified as an incident. The company's "only a state could do this" view and a linked $13.5M crypto-key theft claim are both unverified.
- **AI agents found a zero-day on their own (rising):** Amazon Threat Intelligence says a China-linked actor's Cairn AI agents found and exploited an unknown flaw in MONAI Label, a medical-imaging AI tool, in about 11 minutes. A fix has shipped. Verified by the vendor report and covered by The Record and BleepingComputer. Amazon rates its China attribution "moderate confidence."
- **Gulf GPS jamming peaks (rising):** The GNSS RFI Monitor shows 20-21% of Persian Gulf/Hormuz area-hours affected on Nov 21-22, the worst days in its 30-day window. That's the same water where Iran says tankers ran with AIS off. Independent ADS-B data, but heuristic.
- **Chase, Visa and Mastercard vendor breach (steady):** Chase says attackers reached marketing data (names, emails) held by third-party vendors, and Visa and Mastercard confirmed incidents at providers. The n0n gang claims Qubole was the source, which is unverified (BleepingComputer). Exposure verified by the victims; no payment data reported taken.
- **Pakistan subsea cable fault (falling):** The SEA-ME-WE 5 fault about 11 km off Pakistan slowed internet nationwide. Repairs are due by Dec 6 (Dawn, PTA, NetBlocks). No evidence of sabotage, so treat "cyberattack" posts as unsupported.
- **Hygiene:** If you bank with Chase or hold a Visa or Mastercard, expect phishing that sounds like a breach notice. Go to the bank's app directly; don't use links in emails.

## Home front

Nothing overseas changes home prep. River flooding is the real hazard this week: it crests slowly and can keep rising for days after the rain stops.

## Prep tip

Standard homeowners policies generally don't cover flood, and a new National Flood Insurance Program policy usually has a 30-day waiting period, so it can't be bought once the water is already rising. Action: look up your address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center (msc.fema.gov), then check whether your current policy includes flood coverage.
