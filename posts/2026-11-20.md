## Prep tip: build your own ADS-B "airspace radar"
- Commercial flight trackers filter and delay data. A **$30-60 SDR receiver** (an RTL-SDR or a FlightAware Pro Stick Plus with a built-in 1090 MHz filter) on a Raspberry Pi running **readsb/tar1090** shows the raw 1090 MHz transponder traffic over your area, live and with no account.
- **Antenna:** build a 1090 MHz quarter-wave ground plane: a **69 mm** vertical element plus 4 radials of about 69 mm, angled down 45 degrees, on an SO-239 chassis jack. Put it as high and as clear as you can. Coax loss at 1090 MHz is brutal, so keep runs short or put the receiver up at the mast. Expect **150-250 nm** range with a good line of sight.
- **Why it matters:** a sudden flood of tankers, medevac and government flights, or empty airspace, shows up here before the news covers it. You can also feed ADS-B Exchange and cross-check what officials say.
- **Action:** cut and solder the 69 mm ground plane this weekend, run it for 48 hours, and note your normal traffic. You can only spot "weird" if you know what normal looks like.
