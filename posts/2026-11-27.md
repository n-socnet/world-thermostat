## Prep tip

If you pressure-can low-acid food, check your gauge. Botulism spores need about 240°F, which takes 10 psi on a weighted gauge up to 1,000 ft, or 11 psi on a dial gauge up to 2,000 ft. Higher elevations need more pressure. USDA guidance says to replace a dial gauge that reads more than 2 psi off, and to recheck it every year. Action: have your dial gauge tested (many county extension offices do it free) or switch to a weighted regulator, which doesn't drift.
